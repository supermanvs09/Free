Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This Deep Algorithms Cookie Policy ("Policy") explains how Deep Algorithms uses cookies and similar technologies strictly necessary for providing our services and securing your experience when visiting our website or using our product ("Services").

1.
What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of text stored in your web browser that allow websites and services to function correctly and securely. Cookies help with essential functions like session management, security protection, and enabling navigation across web pages.

You can learn more about cookies generally at https://knowcookies.com

2.
Our Use of Cookies

We are committed to protecting your privacy. As part of that commitment, Deep Algorithms uses only Strictly Necessary cookies - no tracking, no advertising, no analytics. There are no optional cookies on our platform.

3.
Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the website and service to operate properly. They cannot be disabled through our cookie settings, as they are fundamental to the security and core functionality of the Services.

Why do we use them:

To protect websites against bots and automated abuse
To validate legitimate traffic
To maintain session integrity
To ensure proper functionality of security features
4.
Managing Cookies

Since Deep Algorithms only uses essential cookies, there are no cookie preferences for you to configure. You can still control or delete cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling or removing Strictly Necessary cookies may impact on the ability to use certain features or access secured areas of the Services.

Contact Us
