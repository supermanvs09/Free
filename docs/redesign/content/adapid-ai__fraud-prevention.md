Unified Identity Fraud Prevention combines device signals, behavioural intelligence, identity graph intelligence, and real-time transaction context to deliver confident risk decisions across every digital touchpoint, from first login to final payment.

Account Takeover Protection
Stop credential attacks before the session does damage.

Real-time credential abuse and stuffing detection

Real-Time Continuous Risk Scoring
A live risk score across the entire digital banking lifecycle. Not just at login.

adapID AI maintains a live identity risk score for every user, recalculated every second by incorporating behavioural shifts, environmental changes, and cross-channel signals. Threats are caught the moment they emerge — a hijacked session or suspicious transaction — not after the damage is done.

Every-second recalculation: behavioural, device, and cross-channel signals

Mule Account Detection
First-time payees are the highest-risk moment in any transaction.

Mule accounts act as the final layer in fraud chains, receiving and laundering funds before they can be recovered. adapID AI flags mule account indicators at the point of beneficiary addition, validating payees against fraud registries, assessing first-time transfer risk, and scoring the full identity context before funds move.

First-time payee risk scoring with behavioural and registry signals
Real-time payee validation against national fraud databases and watchlists
Mule account pattern detection across onboarding and beneficiary addition

Authorised Push Payment (APP) fraud recovery rates fall below 50% once funds clear. UK Finance, 2024.

/003
Cross-Channel Intelligence
Internet and mobile sessions are not separate events. Neither is fraud.

adapID AI continuously exchanges context between internet banking and mobile channels, establishing semantic relationships between auth events — e.g. a failed internet login correlated with an immediate mobile TOTP request — and computing a unified risk score across all channels.

Continuous context exchange between internet and mobile banking channels
Semantic correlation of cross-channel auth events to detect coordinated attacks
Early detection of SIM-swap-assisted account takeover schemes

Cross-channel gaps are the most exploited vulnerability in digital banking fraud.

THE DECISIONING ENGINE
Five signals. One decision. No blind spots.

Every risk decision fuses five independent intelligence layers simultaneously — not one signal in isolation, not a static ruleset written six months ago. Continuous, converging, and adaptive to each user's unique behavioural baseline.

01 · DEVICE
Device Intelligence
Persistent device fingerprint tracked across every session. Detects emulators, rooted and jailbroken environments, cloned applications, device farm activity, and SIM-swap events, intercepted at the point of contact before authentication completes.
02 · BEHAVIOUR
Behavioural Intelligence
Typing rhythm, gesture velocity, touch pressure, and navigation flow are unique to every legitimate user. Deviations from an individual's baseline are scored in real time, not after the fact.
03 · RISK SCORE
Real-Time Risk Score
A live identity risk score is maintained throughout the entire digital banking lifecycle — not just at login. Recalculated every second, it incorporates new events, behavioural shifts, environmental changes, and cross-channel signals as they emerge. Score range is 0 (no risk) to 100 (maximum risk) with configurable threshold bands. Every contributing signal is logged, explainable, and auditable. Historical score timelines are stored per user for pattern analysis and model tuning.
04 · IDENTITY
Unified Identity Thread
A continuously updated record linking all authentication events, devices, sessions, channels, and risk assessments to a single identity entity. Enables longitudinal fraud pattern detection spanning days, weeks, or months. Context-aware risk decisions leverage the full history of a user's digital interactions — catching slow-burn account takeover attempts that evade event-level detection.
05 · TRANSACTION
Transaction Context
Fuses transactional and behavioural signals mid-session, scoring payment amount, beneficiary history, session deviation, and timing simultaneously to catch manipulation that starts after authentication.
