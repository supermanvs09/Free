Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Privacy Policy Statement

Deep Algorithms provides this Privacy Policy in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

We do not disclose sensitive personal data without prior consent, except as required by law or to trusted service providers under confidentiality obligations. We implement reasonable security practices and procedures to protect personal and sensitive personal data from unauthorised access, use, or disclosure.

1.
Introduction

Deep Algorithms Solution Pvt. Ltd. provides innovative AI-powered security solutions, including adapID AI for behavioral biometrics-based authentication and BotShield AI for intelligent bot detection and prevention.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

2.
Information We Collect

We collect various types of information to provide and improve our services:

Haptics data or HCI data.
IP addresses, user agent details, handshake details.
Traffic patterns, request logs, browser engine information.
3.
How We Use Your Information

We use collected data for:

Identity verification and risk assessment.
Detecting fraudulent behavior and preventing unauthorized access.
Enhancing user experience by providing seamless authentication.
Analyzing and mitigating bot attacks.
4.
Data Sharing and Storage

We do not sell your personal data. Data is stored securely with encryption measures to prevent unauthorized access.

5.
User Rights

You have rights to access, modify, or delete your data as per applicable regulations (DPDPA, GDPR, CCPA, etc.). Requests can be made via our support team.

6.
Security Measures

We implement advanced security protocols, including encryption, access controls, and continuous monitoring to ensure data safety.

7.
Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Users are required to refer this section for the updated policy.

8.
Contact Us

For any queries regarding this policy, contact support@deepalgorithms.in.
