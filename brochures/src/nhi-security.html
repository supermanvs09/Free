Secure the Identities You Can’t Afford to Overlook

Applications, cloud services, APIs and AI agents depend on Non-Human Identities (NHIs). Often hidden, over-privileged and poorly monitored, these identities create security gaps that attackers can exploit.

adapID AI gives every non-human identity an owner, a behavioural baseline and a live risk score, then acts automatically the moment a service account, API key, token or workload identity steps out of pattern.
