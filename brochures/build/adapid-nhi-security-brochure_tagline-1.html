Secure the Identities You Can’t Afford to Overlook

Applications, cloud services, APIs and AI agents depend on Non-Human Identities (NHIs): service accounts, API keys, tokens and workload identities. Often hidden, over-privileged and poorly monitored, these identities create security gaps that attackers can exploit.

How adapID AI helps

adapID AI gives every non-human identity an owner, a behavioural baseline and a live risk score, and acts automatically the moment one steps out of pattern.
