## Situations in the film

Based on public incident reports, without naming the organisations: a stolen password on a remote-access portal with no MFA (a US healthcare network, 2024); a help-desk call that reset an employee's password (a hotel and casino group, 2023); "digital arrest" video-call scams (India, I4C, 2024); an AI chat integration's OAuth tokens used to export customer data (2025); credential stuffing against a login page.
